ZIMSEC AI Teacher: Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026

ZIMSEC AI Teacher is a study helper that runs as a WhatsApp chatbot for ZIMSEC O-Level and A-Level students in Zimbabwe. This page explains what information the service collects, why, and what you can do about it.

We never ask for your EcoCash PIN, password or OTP. Never send them to us or to anyone else. The service has no place to store them.

What we collect

InformationWhy we need it
Your WhatsApp number and WhatsApp profile nameTo reply to you and recognise you when you come back.
Your level (Form 1 to 4, Lower 6, Upper 6)To teach and price at the right level.
Your messages and the last few turns of conversationTo answer your questions and keep the lesson on track.
Practice and mock exam results (subject, topic, score)To mark your work and build your performance report.
Payment records: order reference, amount, EcoCash transaction ID, the number you paid from, timeTo check your payment, switch on your access, issue a receipt, and keep accounts.
Security and audit logs of payment decisionsTo prevent fraud and settle disputes.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or voice notes. The bot asks you to send text only.

How we use it

  • To run the tutor, assignments, mock exams, revision notes and reports.
  • To take and verify EcoCash payments and issue receipts.
  • To send you reminders when your access is about to end. Send STOP to turn these off.
  • To keep the service safe and prevent fraud.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who else handles your information

  • WhatsApp (Meta) carries your messages. Their own privacy policy applies to that part.
  • Supabase hosts our database and servers.
  • Anthropic provides the AI that writes tutor answers. Your question and recent conversation are sent to it so it can reply.

Only the owner and authorised payment administrators can see payment records.

How long we keep it

Study history and chat memory are kept while you use the service, until you erase them. Payment records are kept for accounting and fraud prevention, even after you erase your study data.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Send DELETE in the chat to erase your name, level, study history and chat memory.
  • Send STOP to stop reminders.
  • To ask for anything else, such as a copy or correction of your data, message us on the same WhatsApp number and say what you need.

Payment records cannot be erased on request because we must keep accounts, but they are not used for anything else.

Security

Messages from WhatsApp are checked before we act on them. Student data can be read only by the service itself. Payments are switched on only after an administrator verifies them, and every payment decision is recorded in a log that cannot be edited.

Children

The service is for secondary school students. Students under 18 should use it with the knowledge of a parent or guardian.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Message the ZIMSEC AI Teacher WhatsApp number with the word HELP, or write to us in the chat and say it is about privacy.
